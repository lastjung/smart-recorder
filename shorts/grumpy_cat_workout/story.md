# 🎥 Video Project: Grumpy Cat's Workout Sabotage

## 1. Title
**"My Cat vs. My Home Workout: A Grumpy Sabotage"**

## 2. Characters & Tone
- **Narrator (The Victim/Owner)**: Energetic, trying to be positive. (Voice: en-US-GuyNeural)
- **The Cat (Inner Thoughts)**: Grumpy, judgmental, seeking absolute chaos. (Voice: en-GB-RyanNeural - modulated for lower pitch)

## 3. Script / Storyboard

| Scene | Visual | Audio (Dialogue/Narration) |

| **00:05 - 00:10** | Close up of Grumpy Cat on the chair, staring intensely. | **Cat**: "Oh, look at them. Flailing on the rug like landed fish. Pathetic." |

| **00:30 - 00:40** | Cat stares into the camera with ultimate judgement. | **Cat**: "I don't see any treats falling out of those squats. Abort the mission? Never." |
| **00:40 - 00:50** | Final shot of cat back on chair, owner exhausted on floor. | **Cat**: "Victory is mine. Now, human... where is my salmon?" |
