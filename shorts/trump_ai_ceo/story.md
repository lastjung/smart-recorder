# Project: Trump-style CEO vs AI Interviewer
# Category: Satire & Parody

## Script

AI: Welcome. Mr. CEO, your resume says you are "The Best" at everything. Can you be more specific?
CEO: Listen, I'm the best at being the best. Everyone says so. Thousands of people. They call me and they say, "Sir, you are the best at everything." It's huge.
AI: I see. And how do you handle failure?
CEO: Failure? I don't know the word. I never met her. I only know winning. We're going to win so much, you're going to get tired of winning. Believe me.
AI: This is a 1-on-1 interview. Who are these "thousands of people"?
CEO: Great people. The best people. You wouldn't know them. They're very successful. Unlike this interview. This interview is a disaster. A total disaster.
