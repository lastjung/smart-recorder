# Project: Mars Letter 2050
## Topic: First video letter from Mars in 2050
## Character: Astronaut (En-US-GuyNeural)
## Script:
[00:00 - 00:05] (Scene: Mars surface, red sand)
"Day five hundred and forty-two on the Red Planet."

[00:05 - 00:12] (Scene: Astronaut looking at the sky)
"The dust has finally settled, and for the first time in weeks, I can see home."

[00:12 - 00:20] (Scene: Close up of a small blue dot in the sky or space)
"Earth is just a tiny blue dot from here. It's strange how much you miss the small things."

[00:20 - 00:28] (Scene: Futuristic base interior or lab)
"The smell of rain, the taste of real coffee... and the sound of wind that doesn't scream."

[00:28 - 00:35] (Scene: Beautiful Mars sunset)
"But then I look at this sunset. It's blue, you know. A blue sunset on a red world."

[00:35 - 00:43] (Scene: Astronaut smiling or looking out window)
"We're actually here. We made it. Tell everyone... it's beautiful."
